# V142 Interactive Duo Dance & Formation Engine

- Dynamic stage targets for solo, sync, mirror and duet.
- Frame-rate-independent easing in the Three.js frame loop.
- Persistent secondary dancer during automatic scene switches so its rig is not remounted.
- Soft appearance/disappearance and subtle beat-based duet spacing.
- Existing manual modes, dance director, Foot-IK, retargeting and lighting retained.
- Performance note: the second avatar remains mounted when automatic direction is active, including in solo scenes.
- No physical avatar-to-avatar IK or collision avoidance in this version.
