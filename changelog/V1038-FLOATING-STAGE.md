# V1038 — Floating Stage

## Implemented
- 2 / 4 / 6 low-cost decorative hovering satellite stages outside the stable central avatar deck.
- Section-aware lifts: verse (subtle), build-up (rising), hook and confirmed drop (higher), break (lower).
- Auto, wave, orbit, and rise modes; user-adjustable intensity 0–100%.
- Music energy modulates motion and emission without inventing beat or song sections.
- Pause freezes all positions, reduced motion parks satellite stages in a static safe position.
- All transforms bounded; outer radius below 3.74 world units and no additional WebGL real-time lights.
- New settings propagated through preferences, UI, registry, rendering and stage preview.
- Existing V1037 petal deck, V1034 ring, 360° turntable and performer root unchanged.
- Dependency-free functional tests and isolated TypeScript compilation.
- Manual Linux/Windows CI, with existing V1032 CI reused on pushes to avoid duplicate builds.

**Note:** These satellites are scenic, **not load-bearing stages for dancers**. Moving avatars to a floating platform requires later coordinated animation and Foot-IK work.
