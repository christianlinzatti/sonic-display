# V60 – Settings navigation

- Four tabs: Display, Avatar & Dance, Microphone & Beat, Diagnostics.
- The settings panel is now a large scrollable viewport, including on mobile.
- Settings are accessible before Spotify login and when no track is playing.
- Escape closes the panel; tab buttons expose their pressed state.
- Existing settings and local persistence are preserved.
- Spotify OAuth fixes from the preceding V59 archive are included.
- No dependency installation or full Next.js build was performed.
