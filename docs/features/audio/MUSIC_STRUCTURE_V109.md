# V109 – Music Structure Analyzer / Deutschrap

- Local audio-file analysis uses browser Web Audio decoding, windowed RMS and positive energy transients.
- Beat/BPM estimation is heuristic and may report half- or double-time. User-specified BPM is supported.
- Verse, hook, intro, outro, build, drop and breakdown labels are heuristics; they do not use speech recognition or reliable structural AI classification.
- Deutschrap genre prioritizes head-nod, bounce, trap and groove clip names.
- Analyzed timeline is activated only when the user clicks Apply.
- Spotify playback data does not grant direct audio sample access. No background recording or cloud API.
